QC Photos Guide

Learn how to review quality control photos at the OopBuy warehouse before shipping.

What are QC Photos?

Quality Control photos are taken at the OopBuy warehouse when your items arrive. They show the actual product you received - use them to verify quality before shipping.

How to Review QC

Check stitching, logos, colors, and overall build quality against reference photos. Compare with QC photos in our OopBuy Spreadsheet listings.

Exchange or Return

If QC reveals issues, request an exchange or return through OopBuy before submitting your international shipment. Act quickly - warehouse storage has time limits.

QC Photos Before Shipping

For better OopBuy finds, compare the live source links with warehouse QC photos before adding the item to your shipping checklist. If the color, size tag, material, or visible finish does not match the product page, resolve it before the item is packed for international shipping.
